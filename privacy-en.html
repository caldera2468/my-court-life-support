1. Operator and scope

カルデラ-caldera- (the operator’s public creator name; the “Operator,” “we,” or “us”) explains in this policy how information is handled in My Court Life (the “App”) and its official support website. Contact: caldera2468@gmail.com.

2. Game information stored on your device

The App stores character creation details, such as names, birthday month and day, origin, nationality, height and appearance; teams, abilities, results and career progress; advertising reward usage; settings such as language and volume; and a cache of purchase entitlements. These support gameplay, saved progress, preferences and access to purchased content.

Character details are fictional game settings. You do not need to enter your real name or birth date. A character’s birthday is not used to verify the player’s age. The App has no automatic save-upload feature to a game-operated server. Device or operating-system backups, if enabled, are governed by your settings and the backup provider’s policies.

3. Advertising and Unity technical information

The App uses Unity Ads. Advertising SDKs communicate with external services to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and provide functionality. SDK initialization and ad preparation may occur before you start watching an ad.

Unity’s disclosures describe processing of device and user identifiers; IP addresses and approximate regions derived from them; device, OS, language, screen and connection information; ad impressions, views and clicks; usage and purchase-related information; and performance. Actual processing depends on SDKs, permissions, consent and service settings. We have not added custom code to pass character names or save contents to the advertising SDK.

The bundled Ad Quality component’s privacy declaration lists device identifiers, performance and other diagnostic information for analytics and app functionality. Unity’s engine may also process crashes, runtime information and configuration/device technical data to provide services and improve stability when diagnostic collection is enabled. A bundled component or declaration does not mean every listed item is always transmitted.

See Unity’s privacy policy and player privacy FAQ for its practices and rights-request channels. If an ad takes you to another website or app, that destination’s policies also apply.

4. In-app purchases and Apple StoreKit

Purchases and restoration are processed through Apple StoreKit. The App uses product identifiers, displayed prices, entitlements, transaction identifiers and verification results to confirm purchases, unlock content, restore access and prevent misuse. Purchased-product information is also cached on your device. The game has no custom purchase-management server receiving transaction data.

We do not receive your card number, Apple Account password or payment authentication credentials through the App. See App Store & Privacy for Apple’s handling of transactions, payments, refunds and retention. Sales and transaction reports provided to us by Apple are used for sales administration, accounting and purchase support.

5. Support requests and website

When you email us, we receive your sender address, message, attachments and details you choose to provide, such as device, OS and app versions. We use these to reply, investigate issues, assist with purchases and respond to privacy requests. Send only relevant information. Do not send passwords, verification codes or card numbers.

We receive and manage email through Google Gmail; see Google’s privacy policy. The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement. We have not added our own analytics tags, advertising tags or contact forms to the website.

6. Sharing and international processing

For the purposes described above, Unity, Apple, Google, GitHub and businesses involved in providing their services handle relevant information. Advertising-related sharing is subject to the providers’ policies and applicable consent and permissions. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety, limited to what is needed.

We do not supply local save data or support messages for sale. External services may collect and use information independently according to their roles; they are not all processors acting solely on our instructions. We cannot directly control all third-party processing or deletion. When we entrust or disclose information, we review recipients’ policies and contractual terms and take protective measures required by applicable law. Information may be processed outside your country of residence depending on providers’ facilities and operations. Refer to their policies linked above for their practices and safeguards.

7. Retention and deletion

You can delete a career slot from career selection on the title screen. This removes that slot’s manual save, autosave and in-app backups. It does not delete shared preferences or the purchase entitlement cache. Deleting the App normally removes its local data from that device. Separately manage any OS or personal backups. Offloading an app may preserve its data.

We retain support information for as long as necessary to respond to inquiries or meet legal requirements, and delete it when it is no longer needed. Contact us to request deletion.

Information held by Unity, Apple, Google and GitHub is retained according to their stated purposes, legal requirements and retention criteria. There is no single promised retention period for every SDK or record. Deleting the App or a save does not necessarily delete providers’ records. Use the rights-request channels in Unity’s policy for its information and Apple’s privacy channels for its information. You may also contact us for assistance.

8. Consent, ATT and regional rights

Reading this policy or using the App is not consent to advertising tracking. Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) concerns tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. You can review available permissions under iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Availability varies by iOS version and account conditions. Refusing tracking does not necessarily stop all advertising or basic technical processing.

The App presents an advertising privacy choice before initializing the advertising SDK. Choosing “Allow personalized ads” enables personalization only when the necessary iOS ATT permission is also granted. If you choose “Use non-personalized ads,” or ATT is denied or restricted, the App sends no-consent, opt-out and non-personalized settings to the SDK. Your choice is stored on your device and can be changed in Settings > Other > Change advertising choice in the game. Refusing personalization does not prevent normal play or ad rewards, and does not change the periodic or optional advertising system. Non-personalized ads still process information needed for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Changing your choice does not delete information already collected.

Depending on applicable regional law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data, withdraw consent, or opt out of sale/sharing or targeted advertising. Where applicable in the EEA, UK or similar jurisdictions, the relevant basis may be consent for processing requiring it, contract performance for necessary services, legitimate interests for support and security where applicable, or legal obligations for mandatory retention. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing before withdrawal.

Contact us to exercise rights. We may request the minimum verification needed under applicable law, respond concerning information we hold and direct you to the relevant provider for information it controls. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising applicable rights. You may also have a right to complain to a supervisory authority.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not offered for the App Store Kids Category or primarily directed to children under 13. Legal age thresholds for children’s information and consent vary by region. Parents and guardians should review device purchase and privacy settings. If a parent, guardian or player contacts us about a child’s personal information, we will review and respond under applicable law, including deleting information we hold or directing the request to the relevant provider as appropriate.

10. Security and policy changes

We aim to use safeguards appropriate to the information handled, including limiting access to what is necessary. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. We may revise this policy as features, providers or laws change and update the date on this page. We will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes where applicable law requires it.
