Get in touch

For questions about My Court Life, please email our support address. You can write to us in English or Japanese.

Include “My Court Life” and a short description in the subject line. Explain your question in the email body with as much relevant detail as possible. We will reply by email.

If the email link does not open your mail app, copy the address below and send a message using your preferred email service.

02

Report a bug

Please include the following information, where available, so we can investigate.

  • Your device model, iOS version, and app version
  • The screen and actions involved, including steps to reproduce the issue
  • What you expected to happen and what actually happened
  • When and how often it happens, and the in-game date if known
  • Any error messages or screenshots you can provide

Hide personal information in screenshots. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or credit card details. To protect your save data, contact us before deleting or reinstalling the app to troubleshoot an issue.

03

In-app purchases & restoration

If purchased content is missing, try these steps.

  1. Check that you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
  2. Connect to the internet and open the in-game Club Store. You can also access it through “Additional content / Restore purchases” in Settings.
  3. Select “Refresh store” to retrieve your purchase information again.

If the issue continues, email us with the product name, approximate purchase date, and any message shown in the app. Restoring purchases checks your content entitlements; it does not recover save data.

You can also find purchase restoration guidance on Apple Support.
